An Insider’s Guide to NY Fashion Week

NY Fashion Week can be both amazingly fun & incredibly overwhelming, especially if you have never been. Luckily, I am here to give you an insider's guide and make your first show a pleasurable experience. By taking my tips and tricks and following your gut, you will have an unforgettable experience!

Getting Tickets & Picking the Shows.

If you are lucky or connected, you can get an invite sans the price tag. But I wasn't so lucky or connected in my first two years, so I am here to help you out. First, plan out which shows are most important to you and make a schedule. And this is a biggie: SET A BUDGET. If you are worrying about blowing your cash the whole time, you won't have fun. Check out prices, dates, times, and parties, and see the highlights you want most. Lastly, purchase your tickets and get excited!

Dressing for the Shows

Always be true to your own sense of style. If you don't, you will be uncomfortable the whole time, and it will show. Remember, NY Fashion Week is about expressing yourself and taking in what designers have chosen to express through their new lines. Also, it's important to wear shoes that will be comfortable. Obviously, you want to look good, but you need to be prepared.
